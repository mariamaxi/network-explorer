1. What Is a Computer Network?

Imagine you want to share a picture or a game with your friend. Instead of sending it by traditional mail, you can share it almost instantly using a computer network.

A computer network is a group of computers and other devices connected together to communicate and share information.

Two main types of networks

Wired network

Devices connect using physical cables. Wired connections are usually steady and fast.

Wireless network

Devices connect without cables using Wi-Fi. They are easy to move around.

What happens when you watch an online video? Your device sends a request and the internet sends back a response with the video data.

    4. Artificial Intelligence in Networks

    Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and applications to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

    Learning and making predictions

    AI studies lots of examples and learns patterns. Then it can guess what may happen next, like tomorrow's weather.

    Writing an article

    AI can write a short article about a topic. People should always check the facts.

    Imitating voices

    AI can copy the sound of a voice. This is how some voice assistants can talk.

    Producing videos

    AI can help create video clips from a description or from pictures.

    Predicting network failures

    AI watches network data. If it spots a warning sign, such as a device that keeps slowing down, it can warn engineers before the network stops working.
